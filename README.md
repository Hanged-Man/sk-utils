Jokes aside, yeah, I've been had! Had my fun, fucked around, and found out. Will be moving on to other games, though not before a little message to my fellow knights.

When I came back to this dying little game after 10 years of absence, I found it incredibly difficult to find a reason to keep playing. I'm typically a progression-focused gamer; I usually prefer games where I *feel* like meaningful progress can be made without having to repetitively grind stale content or become a thrift reseller for in-game rarities. Despite SK being perhaps the polar opposite of this, the game has always been near and dear to my heart; the wave of nostalgia that hits me when the log-in screen boots up and the Ready Room loads in is something no other game can deliver, I think.

So I stretched the rules a bit (okay, maybe *a lot*!) -- just to make my own fun. Selfish? Sure. Cheating? Oh, *definitely*. But malicious? Never. At least, I tried my hardest to make sure my shenanigans weren't ruining the fun for most others. I didn't dump items for 1 CR on the AH because I didn't want to completely break the game's item economy. If any merchants were screwed over by the cheap stuff I was putting out, I apologize! The market is yours to make again.

There are many others like me out there, scrambling for reasons and ways to hang onto this adorable old game that has been content-starved for over a decade, before all of us become nothing more than entries on someone's friends list with a `Last Online:` date frozen in time.

For some, the fun is in Haven-sitting and shitposting all day long. For some, the fun is in buying low and selling high (*very* high, in some cases). For some, the fun is in running a fireteam of four knights simultaneously (R.I.P. controller multibox). For some (including some **eminent individuals on KBP and Haven's moderation team**), the fun is in using mods to run a certain dungeon *much* faster than otherwise humanly possible. Anything to stay a little longer. I think it's all very endearing, and it's part of what's kept this game running for so long.

If my ban from KBP and Haven is just because the mod team needed to keep up appearances, then fair enough -- I accept graciously. But to whoever's behind my ban, if you really think you're doing some kind of justice this way, I want you to take a long, hard look at yourself and think about whether what you're doing is the same thing on another scale, in some other shape and form. I don't want to get anyone in trouble, much less removed from the game for any period of time, so rest assured, there won't be a peep from me. But few faults bother me more than hypocrisy.

Rant over! **It's a game. We're all just trying to have fun.**
